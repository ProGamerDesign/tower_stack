🎯 How to Play Tower Stack

  • One-Tap Control: Tap the screen or press Space to drop the sliding block.
  • Trim Overhangs: Any overhang is sliced off cleanly, making your next block narrower.
  • Perfect Combos: Land a drop aligned with the block below (within a few pixels) to keep full width and trigger a combo chime.
  • Size Recovery: Chain 3 perfect drops in a row to expand your block width back out!
  • Game Over: Miss the platform entirely and the round ends. Tap or press space to restart instantly!
